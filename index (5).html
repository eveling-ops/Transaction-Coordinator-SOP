Core rules
Golden rule — A-B / B-C separation

A-B (seller ↔ CHO) and B-C (CHO ↔ buyer) conversations must NEVER cross. Never share seller info with a buyer or buyer info with a seller. You are the only bridge — and nothing crosses.

Cancellation deadlines — mandatory timezone check

One day before DD expires, you must verify the exact cancellation deadline in the property's local timezone. This step is mandatory — do not skip.

  • Read the contract carefully to find the exact cancellation deadline time
  • Open World Time Buddy — this is required, not optional. We operate across multiple timezones and so may you.
  • Add all the states below to your comparison and confirm the deadline against your own current timezone before sending any cancellation

Always have these states loaded in World Time Buddy:

EMD refund tracking

Every time a deal is cancelled and an EMD was submitted, it must be tracked until the money is back. Check the Dead tab on the Transaction Tracker daily:

  • Filter for deals with EMD toggled ON and Refund toggled OFF
  • Follow up with escrow on each one until refund is received
  • Request refund receipt, save in Drive, mark confirmation column on Loan Master List
